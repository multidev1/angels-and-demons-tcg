LIMITED & SPECIAL CARDS

Rare by Design Limited Forever

Some characters exist beyond the regular card pool. Limited Characters are individually numbered collector cards, produced in strictly defined worldwide editions.

ANGELS & DEMONS TCG • SET 1 Limited Edition
Limited Character • Set 1

Malakor

Malakor is the first Limited Character of Angels & Demons TCG. This exclusive edition is strictly limited worldwide, with every individual card receiving its own unique number.

Worldwide Edition 12 COPIES Only 12 Malakor cards will ever be produced worldwide.
Set Set 1
Total Worldwide 12 Copies
Collector Release 10 Copies
Creator Collection 2 Copies
Card Numbering 01/12 – 12/12
Individually Numbered

Every Malakor card is individually numbered. Each copy represents a specific place within the worldwide edition of 12 cards.
