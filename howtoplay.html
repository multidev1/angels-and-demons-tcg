How To Play

Learn the basics of Angels & Demons TCG and prepare for battle.

Table of Contents

1. UNDERSTANDING YOUR CARDS 2. THE FIELD 3. STARTING THE DUEL 4. TURN STRUCTURE 5. SUMMONING MONSTERS 6. BATTLE 7. MAGIC, TRAPS & RELICS 8. SOUL CORES & WINNING

1. UNDERSTANDING YOUR CARDS

Before you begin a duel, it is important to understand the information shown on your cards.

2. THE FIELD

During a duel, cards are placed in different zones on the field.
Each zone has its own purpose and limits.

Monster Zone

You can have a maximum of 5 monsters on your field.

Magic & Trap Zone

You can have a maximum of 5 Magic and Trap cards on your field.

Relic Zone

You can have a maximum of 5 Relics on your field.

Arena Zone

Arena cards are played in a separate Arena zone.
Only 1 Arena card may be active at a time.

Other Zones

Players also have a Hand, Deck, and a shared Graveyard.
Banished cards are removed from the game and cannot be used again unless a card specifically allows it.

3. STARTING THE DUEL

Before the duel begins, both players prepare their decks and draw their starting hands.

Starting the Duel

Decide randomly who starts the duel using a coin or die.

Both players shuffle each other's deck before the duel.

Both players draw 5 cards.

First Turn Restrictions

During the first turn of the duel, neither player may attack.

Special Summons are not allowed during the first turn.

All monsters summoned during the first turn enter the field in Defense mode.

4. TURN STRUCTURE

Each turn is divided into four phases.

1. Draw Phase

Draw 1 card. If you cannot draw because your deck is empty, you lose the duel.

2. Main Phase

During your Main Phase, you can summon monsters, play Magic cards, play Relics, and set Magic and Trap cards according to the rules.

You may play a maximum of 1 Monster card per turn unless a card states otherwise.

You may set or activate a maximum of 3 Magic cards per turn.

3. Battle Phase

Monsters in Attack mode may each attack once.
Both players may activate Magic and Trap cards.

4. End Phase

Your turn ends and play passes to your opponent.

5. SUMMONING MONSTERS

Normal Summon

You may Normal Summon 1 Monster per turn.
A Normal Summon may enter the field face-up in Attack mode or face-down in Defense mode.

Offer Summon

An Offer Summon requires you to sacrifice monsters from your field as a cost.
The sacrificed monsters are sent to the Graveyard.

An Offer Summon does not count as playing an extra monster, and a sacrificed monster is not considered defeated.

Special Summon

A Special Summon allows a monster to enter the field through a card effect or its own summon condition.

A Special Summon does not count as your 1 Monster card per turn.
Special Summons are forbidden during the first turn of the duel.

God Cards

God Cards may only be summoned when the specific conditions stated on the card are fulfilled.

6. BATTLE

During the Battle Phase, monsters in Attack mode can attack.
Each monster may attack once per Battle Phase.

Attacking an Attack Position Monster

The attacker's Attack Power is subtracted from the target's HP.

Attacking a Defense Position Monster

The attacker's Attack Power is subtracted from the target's DEF.

Defense Mode

Monsters in Defense mode cannot attack.

Defeated Monsters

A monster is defeated when its HP or DEF reaches 0.
A monster can be defeated by an attack or by a card effect that destroys it.

Changing Position

A monster may change between Attack and Defense mode once per turn, starting from the second turn that the monster has been on the field.

7. MAGIC, TRAPS & RELICS

Magic Cards

Magic cards are first set face-down on the field.
They may then be activated from the field.

After activation, a Magic card normally goes to the Graveyard unless the card states otherwise.

Trap Cards

Trap cards are set face-down on the field.
They may be activated at any time during either player's turn, including during the same turn they are set.

Relic Cards

Relics are always placed face-up on the field.
They remain on the field until they are destroyed or the monster they are equipped to is defeated, unless the card states otherwise.

A maximum of 3 Relics may be equipped to the same monster.

8. SOUL CORES & WINNING

Starting Soul Cores

Each player starts the duel with 10 Soul Cores.

Losing a Soul Core

You lose 1 Soul Core when a monster is defeated while it is in Attack mode.

You may also lose Soul Cores through specific card effects.

If you have no monsters on the field and your opponent attacks you directly, you lose 1 Soul Core for each attacking monster.

How to Win

You lose the duel when you have 0 Soul Cores.

You also lose when you must draw a card during your Draw Phase but your deck is empty.
