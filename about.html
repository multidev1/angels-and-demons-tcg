ANGELS & DEMONS TCG

ABOUT THE GAME

Choose your side. Build your deck. Battle for Soul Cores.

Enter a world where Angels and Demons clash in strategic battles. Build your deck, command powerful Monsters, use Magic, Traps and Relics, and fight to control the battlefield.

WHAT IS ANGELS & DEMONS TCG?

Angels & Demons TCG is a strategic trading card game where every decision can change the course of a battle.

Build a 50-card deck, summon powerful Monsters and combine different card types to create your own strategy.

Attack your opponent's forces, protect your Soul Cores and use the right cards at the right moment to gain control of the battlefield.

With Angels, Demons, Magic, Traps, Relics, Arena Cards and powerful Gods, there are many ways to approach a duel.

THE BATTLE

Every duel begins with two players, their decks and their Soul Cores. From the first turn onward, players must decide when to summon, when to attack and when to hold their cards for the right moment.

Monsters fight on the battlefield while Magic, Traps and Relics can support your strategy or disrupt your opponent.

Victory is not simply about having the strongest Monster. Reading the battlefield, managing your resources and making the right decisions are what can turn a duel in your favor.

SUMMON

Bring powerful Monsters onto the battlefield and build your forces.

STRATEGIZE

Combine your cards and plan your moves before committing to battle.

BATTLE

Attack opposing Monsters and fight for control of the battlefield.

OVERCOME

Break through your opponent's strategy and fight your way toward victory.

SOUL CORES

Soul Cores are at the heart of every duel.

Players begin the game with 10 Soul Cores. During battle, Soul Cores can be lost through attacks, card effects and other game situations.

Protecting your own Soul Cores while putting pressure on your opponent is a fundamental part of your strategy.

When your opponent's Soul Cores reach 0, you have achieved one of the conditions for victory.

ANGELS & DEMONS

Two opposing forces stand at the center of the game: Angels and Demons.

Their battles shape the world of Angels & Demons TCG, bringing powerful creatures and unique abilities onto the battlefield.

ANGELS

Celestial warriors and powerful beings that fight with the forces of Heaven.

DEMONS

Fierce and dangerous beings that command the powers of Hell and darkness.

THE CARDS

Every card has a role to play. Build your deck by combining different card types and create a strategy that fits your way of playing.

MONSTER

Summon Angels and Demons to the battlefield. Monsters are the main forces that fight your battles.

MAGIC

Use powerful Magic effects to support your Monsters or change the course of a duel.

TRAP

Set Traps and wait for the right moment to respond to your opponent's actions.

RELIC

Equip your strategy with powerful Relics and gain additional effects during the battle.

ARENA

Arena Cards influence the battlefield and can create powerful strategic opportunities.

GOD

Rare and powerful beings capable of making a major impact when they enter the battlefield.

BUILD YOUR DECK

Your deck is your strategy.

Build a 50-card deck using the cards available to you. Choose your Monsters and combine them with Magic, Traps and Relics to create your own approach to battle.

There is no single way to build your deck. Experiment with different combinations, discover powerful interactions and find the strategy that works best for you.

50 CARDS PER DECK
5 MONSTER SLOTS
5 MAGIC / TRAP / RELIC SLOTS
1 ARENA SLOT

THE GODS

Beyond ordinary Monsters stand the Gods.

God Cards represent some of the most powerful beings in Angels & Demons TCG. Their presence can dramatically change the battlefield and force both players to rethink their strategy.

Some Gods also possess a special Bonus Form, giving these extraordinary cards another powerful identity.

Gods are more than powerful cards. They are among the most unique and prestigious cards in the Angels & Demons TCG collection.

THE WORLD OF ANGELS & DEMONS

Heaven and Hell stand on opposite sides of a world filled with powerful creatures, mysterious relics and dangerous magic.

Angels and Demons battle for power, while the cards of the game reveal the creatures, forces and mysteries that make up this universe.

As new cards and sets are introduced, the world of Angels & Demons TCG will continue to grow.
