1. THE DECK AND SOUL CORES

- Each player begins with a Main Deck of 50 cards:
  Monster, Trap, Magic, Relic, and Arena cards.

- Soul Cores are a player's life points. These
  cards do NOT belong to the Main Deck (separate tokens).

- Each player starts with 10 Soul Cores.

- There is no limit to how many Soul Cores a player
  may have.

- A maximum of 5 copies of the same card in a
  Main Deck + Side Deck combined.

- Side Deck: maximum 15 cards, outside the Main Deck.
  Between duels (best-of-3), you may swap 1-for-1.
  The Main Deck must then be exactly 50 again.

2. ZONES

- Field: max. 5 monsters + max. 5 MAGIC/TRAP cards
  + max. 5 RELIC cards.

- Arena does not count toward this.

- Hand, Deck, 1 Shared Graveyard.

- Banished: cards that have been removed from the game.
  They can NO longer be used,
  unless a card explicitly says so.
  (Examples: Summon Paper, Flying Tiger Horse.)

3. START OF THE DUEL

- Decide randomly who starts (coin or die).

- Both players shuffle each other's deck before the duel.

- Both players draw 5 cards.

- FIRST TURN OF THE DUEL (applies to BOTH players):
  * Neither player may attack.
  * No Special Summons.
  * All monsters that are summoned enter the field in
    Defense mode.

4. TURN STRUCTURE

Each turn consists of:

- Draw Phase: draw 1 card. If you cannot draw
  (deck empty) → you lose (deck-out).

- Main Phase:
  * Play a maximum of 1 Monster card per turn,
    unless a card says otherwise.
  * A maximum of 3 Magic cards per turn (set or
    activate).
  * Play Relic cards: they remain on the field
    (max. 5 relics on the field).
  * Set Magic and Trap cards (face-down).
    Setting and activating are two separate steps
    (see section 7).

- Battle Phase: monsters in Attack mode may each
  attack 1×. Both players may activate Magic and
  Trap cards.

- End Phase.

5. SUMMONING MONSTERS

- Normal Summon: 1 per turn. Face-up in Attack mode
  OR face-down in Defense mode (unless the
  first-turn rule says: Defense).

- Offer Summon: sacrifice monsters from your field as a cost
  (they go to the graveyard, unless stated otherwise).
  A offer does not count as "playing an extra monster".
  A monster you sacrifice is NOT "defeated".
  Face-down monsters may be sacrificed; turn them
  face-up at the time of sacrifice.

- SACRIFICE AS A CARD COST: if a card (magic/relic/
  trap) requires a sacrifice as a cost (e.g. Demonic Cake,
  Dragon Contract, Monster Sacrifice, False Lucky Dice), then
  the same applies: the sacrificed monster goes to the
  graveyard and is NOT defeated — NO Soul Core loss,
  no "defeated" triggers. It does not count as "playing
  a monster".

- A SACRIFICE IS ALWAYS FROM THE FIELD, unless the card
  states otherwise. (Exceptions: Malakor are summoned from
  the HAND with their pieces.)

- Face-down cards on the field may be sacrificed;
  they are turned face-up at the time of sacrifice
  (applies to monsters AND magic/trap cards).

- Special Summon: a monster that enters the field
  through a card effect or through its own summon
  conditions (e.g. Summon Paper, Shadow Ambush,
  Second Life, Malakor). A Special Summon does NOT
  count as your 1 monster per turn.
  Special Summons are forbidden during the first turn
  of the duel.
  POSITION: the player chooses whether the monster enters
  in Attack or Defense mode — EXCEPT during the
  first turn of the duel (then Defense).

- Returning monsters from the graveyard requires NO
  sacrifice.

- God Cards: there are God Cards. They may only be
  summoned if their specific conditions stated on
  the card are fulfilled.

- Malakor the Forgotten One: may be summoned
  from the HAND if you have all 6 Malakor pieces AND the
  Malakor card in your hand. Send the 6 pieces
  to the graveyard and summon Malakor (Special
  Summon).

- CONTROL (MIND CONTROL): if you take control over
  an opponent's monster, the controller chooses the
  attacks. If the controlled monster is defeated in
  Attack mode, the ORIGINAL owner loses the Soul Core.
  Equipped Relics remain on the monster. Afterwards,
  the monster returns to its owner.

- BOUNCE (back to the hand): if a monster returns
  to its owner's hand, it is NOT defeated — no
  graveyard, no Soul Core loss, no "defeated" triggers.
  Damage and reduced stats are removed. Equipped Relics go
  to the graveyard (unless the card says otherwise).

6. BATTLE

- The attacker chooses which monster to attack.

- Attack on Attack mode: subtract the attack power
  from the target's HP.

- Attack on Defense mode: subtract the attack power
  from the target's DEF.

- Excess damage is discarded (no damage to the player,
  no backlash for the attacker).

- Face-down monsters: turn face-up before the
  damage calculation (position = Defense).

- HP and DEF remain reduced until a card effect
  restores them.

- Defense mode CANNOT attack.

- Monsters may attack once they are on the field
  (no summoning sickness).

- If a monster CANNOT be attacked (e.g.
  Shield of Gods), it also cannot be chosen
  as the target of card effects — unless the card says otherwise.

- Change position (Attack ↔ Defense): 1× per turn,
  starting from the second turn that the monster has been on the field.

- Monsters with 2 attack values must use a different
  move each turn, unless a card says otherwise.

- DEFEATED: a monster is defeated when its HP
  or DEF reaches 0 — through damage from an attack OR through
  a card effect that destroys it. (Destroyed =
  defeated.)

- YOU LOSE 1 SOUL CORE WHEN:
  1) A monster (ANY monster, including GOD-race) is
    defeated — by attack OR by a card effect —
    WHILE it is in Attack mode.
  2) A special card destroys a Soul Core.
  3) You have no monsters on the field and your
    opponent attacks you directly: 1 Soul Core
    per attacking monster.

7. CARD TYPES

- MAGIC: is FIRST set face-down on the field
  (not activated = face-down). It may then
  be activated from the field (turn face-up).
  After activation, it resolves and goes to the graveyard,
  unless stated otherwise (persistent magics such as
  Angel/Demon Ascension remain on the field).
  TIMING:
  * On YOUR OWN turn: you may set and activate a magic
    during the same turn.
  * On the OPPONENT'S turn: you may only
    activate magics that are already face-down on the field
    (you cannot set a new magic during the opponent's
    turn).
  Activation is allowed in the Main and Battle Phase.
  A magic card counts toward the limit of 5
  magic/trap cards as long as it is on the field
  (face-down or face-up).

- TRAP: set face-down. May be activated at any time
  during either player's turn, INCLUDING during the
  same turn it is set.

- RELIC: always face-up on the field. Remains there
  until it is destroyed or the monster to which it is equipped is defeated.
  * A maximum of 3 Relics on the same monster.
  * If the monster leaves the field (defeated,
    sacrificed, returned to the hand), the
    Relics go to the graveyard — unless the card
    says otherwise.

- ARENA: play to a separate Arena zone. Only 1 Arena
  card may be active at a time; a new
  Arena card replaces the old one. Arena cards
  do not count toward the limit of 5 non-monsters.

8. TIMING AND RESPONSES

- AFTER EVERY ACTION, the opponent may respond with a
  Trap or Magic card before the action is
  resolved. Actions: summon monster, play magic,
  play relic, set or activate trap, change
  position, declare attack.

- TICK EFFECTS ("each turn", burn, poison,
  ascension, etc.): these trigger at the START of the
  Battle Phase of the player who controls the effect.

- Attack steps: choose attacker → choose target →
  opponent may respond (Trap/Magic) →
  calculate damage → resolve effects → next
  attack.

- If both players want to respond at the same time, the
  player whose turn it is resolves their effects first.

- A monster's effects take effect IMMEDIATELY
  when it is on the field, unless stated otherwise
  on the card or unless the card is face-down (then
  the effect only becomes active when the card is turned
  face-up).

9. HAND, FIELD, AND VISIBILITY

- There is no maximum to the number of cards a
  player may have in hand.

- If a card restricts a player's hand size
  (e.g. Curse of Hand: max. 5 cards for X
  turns), that player discards down to the maximum at the START of
  their turn. The discarded cards
  go to the graveyard.

- Cards on the field do not need to be played
  immediately. A player may place cards face-down on
  the field to keep their strategy hidden.

- Visibility by card type:
  * Monsters: face-up (Attack/Defense) OR face-down
    (in Defense until attacked or flipped).
  * Magic: set (face-down until activation) — only
    becomes face-up at the time of activation. (On your
    own turn, you may set and activate it immediately.)
  * A face-down Magic and a face-down Trap are NOT distinguishable
    from one another — both are simply face-down cards on
    the field.
  * Trap: set (face-down until activation).
  * Relic: always face-up.

- It is forbidden to view the opponent's cards
  in hand or deck, unless a card says so.

10. TERMINOLOGY

- Turn = Round = one player's turn.
  (The two words mean the same thing; card text
  may use both.)

- Battle = Duel = one game (does not count per turn or
  per round).

- SUMMONED = ALL ways in which a monster enters
  the field: Normal Summon, Offer
  Summon AND Special Summon.

- Cards in the Graveyard may no longer be used,
  unless a card effect says so. Cards can only return
  from the Graveyard to play if another card states this.

11. WINNING AND LOSING

- You lose when:

  * You have 0 Soul Cores.

  * You must draw a card, but your deck is empty
    (deck-out during your Draw Phase).

SIDE DECK

- Maximum 15 cards, outside the Main Deck.

- The Side Deck may contain Monster, Magic, Trap, and
  Relic cards.

- Between duels, you may swap 1-for-1: for every card
  you remove from the Main Deck, you must place one back
  from the Side Deck.

- The Main Deck must be exactly 50 after swapping.

- You may not use the Side Deck during a duel,
  unless a card or rule says so.
