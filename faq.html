ANGELS & DEMONS TCG

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Angels & Demons TCG.

GENERAL

What is Angels & Demons TCG?

Angels & Demons TCG is a strategic physical trading card game where Angels and Demons battle for power.

Players build their own decks, summon Monsters, use Magic, Traps and Relics, and fight to reduce their opponent's Soul Cores.

How many cards are currently available?

The current collection contains 206 cards, including 201 regular cards and 5 bonus cards.

New cards and sets may be introduced in the future.

Is Angels & Demons TCG a physical card game?

Yes. Angels & Demons TCG is a physical tabletop trading card game designed to be played with real cards.

GETTING STARTED

How do I start playing?

Start by building a 50-card deck and preparing your Soul Cores.

For a complete explanation of the game, visit the How To Play page and read the TCG Rules.

How many cards are in a deck?

Each player's deck contains 50 cards.

How many Soul Cores do I start with?

Each player starts the duel with 10 Soul Cores.

How many Monsters can I have on the field?

You can have a maximum of 5 Monster Cards on your field at the same time.

CARD TYPES

What types of cards are there?

Angels & Demons TCG features Monster, Magic, Trap, Relic, Arena and God Cards.

What are Monster Cards?

Monster Cards are the creatures that fight on the battlefield.

Angels and Demons are represented as Monster Cards.

What are Magic Cards?

Magic Cards provide special effects that can support your strategy or affect the battle.

What are Trap Cards?

Trap Cards allow you to respond to your opponent's actions and can create unexpected changes during a duel.

What are Relic Cards?

Relic Cards provide special effects that can support your strategy during the battle.

What are Arena Cards?

Arena Cards influence the battlefield and can create special strategic opportunities.

Only 1 Arena Card can be active at a time.

What are God Cards?

God Cards represent extremely powerful beings within the world of Angels & Demons TCG.

Their arrival on the battlefield can have a major impact on the course of a duel.

GAMEPLAY

Who goes first?

The starting player begins the duel, but cannot attack during their first turn.

How many Monster Cards can I summon per turn?

Normally, you can play 1 Monster Card per turn.

Some card effects may allow additional summons when specifically stated on the card.

How many Magic, Trap and Relic Cards can I have on the field?

You can have a maximum of 5 Magic, Trap and Relic Cards combined on your field.

Can Monsters attack more than once per Battle Phase?

Normally, each Monster can attack a maximum of once during each Battle Phase.

Can a Monster in Defense Mode attack?

No. A Monster in Defense Mode cannot attack.

How do I win a duel?

There are multiple ways for a player to lose a duel.

One of the main victory conditions is reducing your opponent's Soul Cores to 0.

For all victory and defeat conditions, see the complete TCG Rules.

SOUL CORES

What are Soul Cores?

Soul Cores are a central resource in Angels & Demons TCG and are directly connected to victory and defeat.

Can I lose Soul Cores during battle?

Yes. Soul Cores can be lost through certain attacks, card effects and other game situations.

COLLECTING

What rarities are available?

Angels & Demons TCG features several rarities, including Common, Rare, Legendary and God.

What is a 1st Edition card?

1st Edition cards are part of the first edition of the Angels & Demons TCG collection.

The 1st Edition mark identifies these cards as belonging to the first edition.

What are Bonus Forms?

Some powerful God Cards have a special Bonus Form.

These forms represent another version of the God and are part of the special God Card collection.

Are all cards limited?

Not all cards have a limited print quantity.

Special cards may have specific print or availability limits.

Is there a limited God Card?

Yes. Certain God Cards are specially limited.

Details about specific limited cards and their availability will be announced officially.
